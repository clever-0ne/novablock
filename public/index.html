FAQ

Answers to common questions

How do I create an account?

Click “Open your account”, enter your name, email and a password, then confirm your email with the verification code we send you.

How do I deposit funds?

Deposits are made in Bitcoin, USDT or Ethereum. After you submit a deposit it appears as pending in your Transactions and is credited to your balance once approved.

How do withdrawals work?

Request a withdrawal from your dashboard. It shows as pending until it is reviewed and approved, and you can follow its status in your transaction history.

Why do I need to verify my identity?

Verification protects your account and unlocks the full platform. It has three levels: email confirmation, then identity documents, then proof of address.

How does the referral program work?

Every account gets a personal referral link. Share it, and people who sign up through it are linked to your account so you can track your network from the Referrals page.
